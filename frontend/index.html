    What is simulated?

    The weekday menu is repeated across generated weeks. A 7-day report requires at least one student plan response for every scheduled meal on every report date; it will not fill missing attendance with the generated baseline. Submitted plans drive 70–80% of attendance forecasts and the generated model supplies the rest. Suggested servings add an 8% reserve. Simulated waste assumes cooked servings of 0.38 kg for breakfast, 0.60 kg for lunch, 0.18 kg for snacks, and 0.55 kg for dinner, plus generated preparation and plate-leftover allowances. None are measured mess data. Sunday lunch is blank in the supplied plan.

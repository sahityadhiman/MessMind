What is simulated?

The weekday menu is repeated across eight generated weeks, starting with 1,000 eligible students. Suggested servings add an 8% reserve. Simulated waste assumes cooked servings of 0.38 kg for breakfast, 0.60 kg for lunch, 0.18 kg for snacks, and 0.55 kg for dinner, plus a generated 6%–11% preparation reserve and 20–55 g of plate leftovers. None are measured mess data. Sunday lunch is blank in the supplied plan.
